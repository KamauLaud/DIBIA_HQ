THE UNIT OF WORK

Research moves in three-person cells.

A cell forms around one problem — academic or industry — and is carried by three, guided by one to two Jonin- or Kage-level researchers. Small enough to move without noise, complete enough to carry a study end to end.

Our ninja-netrunners work both sides of the aisle, so a cell can chase a peer-reviewed paper or a client deliverable with the same rigor. When the mission ships, the cell dissolves back into the village. Knowledge stays; hierarchy doesn’t.

THE CRAFT — HOW THE TASK FORCE WORKS
Ninjutsu = nethacks

“No new physics — new seals.”

A jutsu is composition. Our ninjutsu are netrunner-style nethacks: cobbling together technology that already exists to perform a function it never had. The craft is knowing which seals to chain — and refusing the ones that take without asking.

Research ≠ slow engineering

“Different job. Different clock. Different definition of done.”

Engineering ships what is known; research earns what isn’t — the long, high-risk bets that open the field. The village runs both without confusing them: odd jobs pay the day, deep bets change the default.
