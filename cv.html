Read the journey as a list
  1. SEP 2010 — MAY 2016B.S. & M.S., Computer Engineering · Rochester Institute of Technology. Magna cum laude. M.S. on gaze estimation and computer vision. With Andreas Savakis.
  2. SEP 2012 — MAY 2014Teaching Assistant · RIT. Digital Systems, Assembly Language, Intro to Computer Engineering; mentored underclassmen.
  3. SEP 2013GEM M.S. Fellowship · National GEM Consortium. M.S. fellowship, 2013–2016.
  4. SEP 2016 — AUG 2019SoC Design Engineer & CV Applied Research Scientist · Intel. Pre-silicon validation for GPU IPs. Built a real-time ML pipeline that found sensitive information in data streams and stripped privacy identifiers, which led to several patent initiatives. Trained 30+ senior engineers on the validation pipeline.
  5. MAR 2019Technical white paper, Strata Data Conference · Intel. The privacy-identifier pipeline, presented at Strata Data 2019.
  6. SEP 2019 — JUN 2026Ph.D., Computer Science · Graduate Research Assistant · Virginia Tech · 3D Interaction Group. Gaze-driven, privacy-aware, context-aware interaction in XR. A privacy–utility design space (avoidance → mitigation → protection), studies up to N=206, and teaching support throughout. With Doug A. Bowman.
  7. SEP 2019GEM Ph.D. Fellowship · National GEM Consortium. Full Ph.D. fellowship.
  8. MAR 2021Virtual Loupes · IEEE VR Workshops 2021. An augmented reality aid for microsurgery. With Doug A. Bowman.
  9. MAY 2021 — AUG 2021AR/VR Research Intern · Adobe Research. Lead developer and study designer: notification delivery in VR/AR, keeping messages understandable while distracting users as little as possible. Became GoldiLocks Zoning. With Stephen DiVerdi.
  10. MAR 20221st of 17 teams, 3DUI Contest · IEEE VR 2022. Clean the Ocean: new modifications to Go-Go and World-in-Miniature techniques.
  11. MAR 2022BuildAR · IEEE VR Workshops 2022. A proof-of-concept prototype of intelligent AR in construction. With Doug A. Bowman, Joseph Gabbard.
  12. MAY 2022 — SEP 2022Camera-Hardware Research Intern · Apple. Ran a perceptual user study tying camera-hardware design specs to the end-user experience on Apple Vision Pro, and turned the findings into hardware recommendations.
  13. OCT 2024GoldiLocks Zoning · ACM SUI 2024. Gaze alone, read as passive context, drives task-agnostic VR notification placement. With Stephen DiVerdi, Doug A. Bowman.
  14. NOV 2024Towards Intelligent AR · arXiv 2411.02684. A taxonomy of context, an architecture, and an empirical study. With Shakiba Davari, Daniel Stover, Alexander Giovannelli, Doug A. Bowman.
  15. JUN 2026Hidden in Plain Gaze · Manuscript, 2026. An engineered gaze representation keeps ~85% of task utility while cutting re-identification ~10× across 206 identities.
  16. JUN 2026Dissertation defended · Virginia Tech. Protection Behind the Veil: Gaze, Privacy, and Context-Aware Adaptation in Extended Reality. With Doug A. Bowman.
  17. JUN 2026 — NOWFounder & guildmaster · DIBIA. An independent research village: consultancy, marketplace, incubator. Three-person cells on every mission.
  18. JUL 2026The Purple Flame · SSRC Just Tech Fellowship application. The myth and the mechanism: a 5-minute short plus a 2027 build-year work proposal.
  19. OCT 2026Privacy on Autopilot · IEEE ISMAR 2026. More assistant autonomy moved users into a supervisory “auditor” role: detection accuracy went up with no measured cost to workload or trust (N=30).
  20. NOV 2026 — DEC 2027SpatialSENSai: a context-sensing layer for XR · DIBIA · trajectory. A portable Unity/Unreal layer that fuses intrinsic and egocentric signals into a privacy-preserving context stream, so the privacy posture travels with the app.
